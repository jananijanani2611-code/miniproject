📋 Tourist Safety Guidelines

✅ Before Your Trip

  • Register at the tourist entry point
  • Collect your IoT tracking device
  • Install the Smart Tourist app on your phone
  • Share your itinerary with family/friends
  • Check weather forecasts
  • Pack appropriate clothing and gear

📱 Using the IoT Device

  • Keep it charged: Charge your device daily
  • Keep it ON: Never switch off the tracking device
  • Carry it always: Don't leave it in your hotel
  • SOS Button: Red button for emergencies only
  • Test it: Ensure it's working before trekking

🥾 During Trekking

  • Stay on marked trails
  • Don't venture into restricted areas
  • Trek in groups, never alone
  • Inform someone about your route
  • Carry water, snacks, and first aid
  • Respect local wildlife and environment

🚨 In Case of Emergency

  • Press SOS: Hold the red button for 3 seconds
  • Stay calm: Don't panic
  • Stay visible: Don't hide in dense areas
  • Save battery: Reduce phone usage
  • Wait for help: Rescue team will be dispatched

⚠️ What NOT to Do

  • ❌ Don't switch off your tracking device
  • ❌ Don't trek during heavy rain or fog
  • ❌ Don't ignore warning signs
  • ❌ Don't separate from your group
  • ❌ Don't consume alcohol while trekking
  • ❌ Don't litter - keep hills clean
