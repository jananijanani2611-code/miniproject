How to Use Smart Tourist Safety App

Follow these simple steps to stay safe during your travel

👤 Tourist Login

Create an account or login as a tourist to access safety features, live location tracking, and SOS services.

🗺️ Live Map

View safe zones and danger areas in real time. The map helps you choose safer routes while travelling.

🚨 SOS Button

In case of emergency, press the SOS button. Your live location will be instantly sent to the admin for help.

🛡️ Admin Monitoring

Admin receives SOS alerts with location details and can take quick rescue action immediately.
