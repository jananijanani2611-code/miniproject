📖 About Smart Tourist Safety System

🎯 Our Mission

To ensure the safety of tourists visiting hill stations through real-time monitoring, AI-powered risk assessment, and instant emergency response systems.

🌟 Why Ooty?

Ooty, known as the "Queen of Hill Stations," attracts thousands of tourists annually. However, the hilly terrain, unpredictable weather, and remote areas pose safety challenges. Our system addresses these concerns.

💡 How It Works

  • Registration: Tourists register at entry points and receive IoT tracking devices
  • Real-time Tracking: GPS monitors tourist locations continuously
  • AI Risk Assessment: System analyzes signal loss duration and categorizes risk levels
  • Emergency Response: One-click SOS alerts notify admin and authorities immediately
  • Blockchain Security: All data is secured with blockchain hashing for integrity
