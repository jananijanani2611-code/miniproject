🤖 AI Risk Assessment System

How Our AI Works

Our intelligent system continuously monitors tourist safety by analyzing GPS signal duration and location data. The AI automatically categorizes risk levels to ensure rapid response.

Risk Level Categories

🟢 GREEN - Safe Zone

Signal Lost: 0-10 minutes

Status: Tourist is safe and location is being tracked

Action: Continue normal monitoring

🟡 YELLOW - Warning Zone

Signal Lost: 10-30 minutes

Status: Potential risk detected

Action: Monitor closely and prepare response team

🔴 RED - Danger Zone

Signal Lost: 30+ minutes

Status: High danger - Immediate action required

Action: Deploy rescue team immediately

🔍 What Triggers Risk Assessment?

  • GPS signal loss duration
  • Movement patterns
  • Location history
  • Manual SOS button press
  • Device inactivity

📊 AI Benefits

  • Real-time Analysis: Instant risk detection
  • Automated Alerts: No manual monitoring needed
  • Predictive Safety: Prevent incidents before they occur
  • Data-Driven: Decisions based on patterns and history
